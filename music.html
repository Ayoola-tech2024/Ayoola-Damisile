How Music Informs Software Engineering

Classical polyphony requires playing 4 to 5 independent musical voices simultaneously with distinct phrasing and rhythm. This intense pattern recognition and structural discipline translates directly into software engineering: designing decoupled microservice architectures, managing asynchronous execution threads, and writing clean, maintainable code.
