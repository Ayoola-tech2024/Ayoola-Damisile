How Music Informs Software Engineering

Classical polyphony requires playing independent musical voices simultaneously with distinct phrasing and rhythm. This pattern recognition and structural discipline translates directly into software engineering: designing decoupled microservice architectures, managing asynchronous execution threads, and writing clean code.
